# Product Price Template

`ECOTech_ProductPriceTemplate.xlsx` — the 10-sheet template, filled for ECO CO.

Use it as the **reference format** when onboarding a new product: give it to
Claude alongside the new product's configurator so the output matches this
structure exactly.

Sheets: Product · AttributeDefinition · Product and Attributes · AttributePicklist
· AttributePicklistValue · Product to Product Constraints · Product Internal
Constraint · Configurable Product Pricing · Non Configurable Product Pricing ·
Bundle Products.
